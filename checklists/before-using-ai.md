# Before Using AI

Use this checklist before asking an AI system to complete a meaningful task.

---

## Clarify the Goal

- [ ] What decision or outcome am I actually trying to achieve?
- [ ] Am I solving the right problem?
- [ ] Is AI the appropriate tool for this task?

---

## Provide Context

- [ ] What information does the AI not know?
- [ ] What assumptions am I making?
- [ ] What constraints should the AI consider?

---

## Preserve Human Judgment

- [ ] Which parts of this task require my expertise?
- [ ] What should not be delegated to AI?
- [ ] What values or priorities should guide the final decision?

---

## Plan for Verification

- [ ] Which claims require independent verification?
- [ ] How will I evaluate the quality of the response?
- [ ] Who is responsible for the final decision?

---

**Remember:** AI can assist with thinking, but it cannot replace responsibility.
