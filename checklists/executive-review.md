# Executive AI Review Checklist

Use this checklist before making organizational decisions supported by AI.

---

## Strategic Alignment

- [ ] Does this support organizational goals?
- [ ] Have success criteria been defined?
- [ ] Are the tradeoffs understood?

---

## Risk

- [ ] What could go wrong?
- [ ] Who is most affected by failure?
- [ ] Have legal, ethical, and reputational risks been considered?

---

## Stakeholders

- [ ] Have all relevant perspectives been considered?
- [ ] Who benefits?
- [ ] Who carries the risk?

---

## Human Oversight

- [ ] What decisions remain human decisions?
- [ ] Who owns accountability?
- [ ] What will be monitored after implementation?

---

The goal is not to approve AI-generated recommendations automatically, but to strengthen human judgment before acting.
