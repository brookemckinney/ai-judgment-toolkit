# Before Publishing AI-Assisted Work

Review this checklist before sharing AI-assisted content externally.

---

## Accuracy

- [ ] Have I verified factual claims?
- [ ] Are sources accurate and current?
- [ ] Are statistics correctly represented?

---

## Judgment

- [ ] Does this reflect my actual position?
- [ ] Have I challenged AI-generated assumptions?
- [ ] Have I added my own expertise or perspective?

---

## Quality

- [ ] Is the writing clear?
- [ ] Does the structure make sense?
- [ ] Would I confidently put my name on this work?

---

## Ethics

- [ ] Does the work fairly represent people and ideas?
- [ ] Could anyone be harmed by inaccuracies?
- [ ] Have I considered unintended consequences?

---

Publishing AI-assisted work means taking responsibility for the final product—not just the prompt.
