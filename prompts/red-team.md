# Red Team Prompt

## Purpose

Use this prompt to deliberately challenge an AI-generated idea, recommendation, or plan.

The objective is to expose weaknesses before implementation.

---

## Prompt

```text
Act as a constructive critic.

Review the proposal below and identify:

1. Weak assumptions
2. Missing evidence
3. Logical gaps
4. Hidden risks
5. Failure scenarios
6. Stakeholders who may disagree
7. What would make this proposal unsuccessful

Do not improve the proposal yet.

Only identify vulnerabilities.
```

---

## Why It Works

AI often defaults toward agreement and refinement.

A deliberate "red team" review introduces productive skepticism and reduces overconfidence.
