# Evidence Evaluation Prompt

## Purpose

Use this prompt to separate verified information from assumptions and uncertainty.

---

## Prompt

```text
Evaluate the following claim without assuming it is true.

Separate your response into:

- What can be established from available evidence
- What is plausible but unverified
- What is inferred
- What information is missing
- What evidence would change the conclusion

Clearly flag uncertainty.
```

---

## Why It Works

Fluent language can sound convincing even when evidence is weak. This prompt helps slow the evaluation process and make uncertainty visible.
