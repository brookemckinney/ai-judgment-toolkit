# Decision Support Prompt

## Purpose

Use this prompt when AI is helping with a decision that still requires human judgment.

The goal is to make criteria, tradeoffs, uncertainty, and responsibility visible before choosing a path.

---

## Prompt

```text
Act as a decision-support partner, not a decision-maker.

For the decision below:

1. Identify the relevant criteria
2. Surface meaningful tradeoffs
3. Describe risks and uncertainties
4. Present at least three viable options
5. Explain what values or priorities would favor each option
6. Identify what must remain a human decision

Do not select an option unless I explicitly request a recommendation.
```

---

## Why It Works

AI can compare options quickly, but it cannot decide which values should matter most.

This prompt keeps the model in a supporting role while preserving human responsibility for the final choice.
