# Problem Framing Prompt

## Purpose

Before asking AI to solve a problem, first examine how the problem has been framed.

Many poor AI outputs begin with poorly framed questions.

---

## Prompt

```text
Before proposing a solution, help me examine how I have framed the problem.

Identify:

1. Assumptions embedded in my description
2. Missing stakeholders
3. Missing context
4. Terms requiring clarification
5. Alternative ways to frame the problem

Do not solve the problem yet.
```

---

## Why It Works

AI is very good at answering questions.

Human judgment is deciding whether the question itself is the right one.
