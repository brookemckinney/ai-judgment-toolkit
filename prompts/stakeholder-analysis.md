# Stakeholder Analysis Prompt

## Purpose

Use this prompt before making decisions that affect multiple people or groups.

The goal is to uncover perspectives, incentives, and unintended consequences that may otherwise be overlooked.

---

## Prompt

```text
Analyze this situation from the perspective of each stakeholder.

For each stakeholder, identify:

1. Their goals
2. Their concerns
3. What success looks like for them
4. What risks they perceive
5. What information they may have that others do not

Then identify:

- Stakeholders that appear underrepresented
- Potential conflicts
- Shared interests
- Questions that should be answered before moving forward

Do not recommend a solution until every stakeholder has been considered.
```

---

## Why It Works

Many poor decisions are not caused by bad reasoning—they are caused by missing perspectives.

This prompt helps broaden the conversation before narrowing toward a decision.
