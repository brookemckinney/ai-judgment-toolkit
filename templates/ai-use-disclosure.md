# AI Use Disclosure Template

This template can be adapted for reports, presentations, articles, research, or internal documentation.

---

## Example

Artificial intelligence was used during the development of this work to support brainstorming, drafting, editing, and organizational tasks.

All factual claims, interpretations, conclusions, and final decisions were reviewed and approved by the author.

Responsibility for the final work remains entirely with the author.

---

## Short Version

AI assisted in preparing this work.

Final judgment, verification, and responsibility remain with the author.

---

## Internal Team Version

AI was used as a decision-support tool during preparation.

Human contributors remained responsible for:

- verifying information
- interpreting results
- making decisions
- approving final outputs

---

This disclosure should be adapted to match organizational policy.
